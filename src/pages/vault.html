How the vault protects you

  • Your passphrase turns into an encryption key with 600,000 rounds of PBKDF2 — in your browser, never sent.
  • Every item is encrypted with AES-256-GCM before it leaves your device.
  • We store only scrambled data. Even with full access to our server, nobody can read your vault.
  • It locks itself after 10 minutes without activity.

Keep two things safe

Your vault passphrase and your recovery key each open the vault on their own. If both are lost, nobody can recover the data — that’s what zero-knowledge means.

Why a zero-knowledge vault?

Most online “vaults” can read what you store: they hold the keys. Here, the key is created and kept on your device, so a database leak, a rogue admin or a court order would only ever reveal ciphertext. Turn on two-factor sign-in as well, so nobody can even reach your encrypted data without your phone.
