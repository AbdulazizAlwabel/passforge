About Nicely Done

Hi, I’m Abdulaziz.

I make these tools on my own — no company, no investors, no ads. Here’s why, what I promise you, and how you can check that I keep my word.

01

Why I built this

A

Most tool websites aren’t honest.

You find a site to shrink a PDF or turn a photo into a document. You spend ten minutes on it — and then, right at the end, it asks you to pay, or to sign up, or it simply won’t let you download your own file. Along the way it has uploaded your contract or your ID to some computer you know nothing about.

That really annoyed me. So I took the matter into my own hands and built the tools myself — the way I think they should work: free, simple, and on your own device.

It began with one password generator I could trust for my own accounts. Today it’s a whole set of tools, and I keep adding the ones people ask for.

02

My promise to you

  • Free, for real. No “pay to download” at the end, no trial that runs out, no premium version hiding the good bits.
  • Your files stay yours. PDFs, photos and anything you type are handled on your phone or computer. They’re not uploaded to me or anyone else.
  • No ads, no tracking. No analytics, no tracking cookies, no advertising scripts. I don’t know who visits, and I don’t want to.
  • No account needed. Every tool works without signing up. An account is only for the extras that need one, like the vault.
  • Honest about limits. If a tool can’t do something, it says so.
