Good to know

Conversions use the official definitions — one pound is exactly 0.45359237 kg and one inch exactly 2.54 cm. Type in either box to convert either way.

“A gigabyte” means two different things: phone and drive makers count in thousands (GB), computers often count in 1,024s (GiB). That’s why a 128 GB phone shows less space than you expected. Both kinds are here.
