Umm al-Qura

Conversions use the Umm al-Qura calendar — the official calendar of Saudi Arabia — built into your browser via Intl. Supported range: 1300–1600 AH (1882–2174 CE).

Note

Religious observances such as Ramadan and Eid are set by moon sighting and can differ from the computed calendar by a day.

Two calendars, one view

The Hijri calendar is lunar: its 12 months have 29 or 30 days, so a Hijri year is about 11 days shorter than a Gregorian year, and Hijri dates drift through the seasons over a 33-year cycle. Use the dual calendar to see both systems side by side for any month.
