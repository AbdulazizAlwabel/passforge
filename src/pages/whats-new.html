What’s new

What I’ve been building lately, newest first. Most of these started as ideas from people like you.

October 2026

Email checker: is it really from your bank?

Paste a suspicious email — or its full source — and see who really sent it, where its links really go, and the pressure tricks inside, in Arabic and English. It reads the sender checks your email provider already did (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), flags dangerous attachments, and never uploads the email. The link checker now also uses URLhaus, a list of links that spread malware.

October 2026

Two new tools: QR codes and photo resizing

A QR code maker for links, Wi-Fi, WhatsApp and contacts — the codes hold your details directly, so they never expire. And a photo resizer that shrinks pictures for email or upload limits, right on your device, leaving out the hidden location data. The live password card is back on the home page, every tool now explains itself in three steps, and links you share show a proper preview.

October 2026

Smoother, with a bit of personality

When you open a tool from a card, the card grows into the page. Downloads, copies and pins now show a little “done” moment, and the home page got a few hand-made touches. If your phone is set to reduce motion, all of it stays still.

Try things right on the home page

Test a password, grab a new one, or see today’s Hijri date and how long until Ramadan — without opening a page. Pin your favourite tools with ☆ so they wait for you on the home page. The link checker now takes a link apart and colours the suspicious bits, and the strength checker shows how long your password would hold out, from seconds to the age of the universe.
