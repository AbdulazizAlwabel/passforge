About the lookup

Your public IP can only be seen from outside your network, so this page asks a public geolocation service (ipwho.is, falling back to ipapi.co) once on load. Everything else, including the subnet calculator, runs locally.

Tip

Type CIDR directly — 10.0.0.0/8 — or drag the slider. Click any result to copy it.
