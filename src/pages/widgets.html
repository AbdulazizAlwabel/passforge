Hijri date

Today in the Umm al-Qura calendar beside the Gregorian date, with a countdown to the next Ramadan or Eid. Arabic or English, small or medium, plus Lock Screen styles. Tap it to open the date converter.

How to

Install in a minute

  1. Install the free Scriptable app from the App Store.
  2. On your iPhone, open this page in Safari and tap Get widget, then choose Open in Scriptable. If that option doesn’t appear, tap Copy code, create a new script in Scriptable with +, paste, and give it a name.
  3. Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap + (or Edit → Add Widget), search for Scriptable and pick a size.
  4. Tap the new widget, choose your script under Script, and optionally add a Parameter. For the Lock Screen, add a Scriptable widget from the Lock Screen editor the same way.

The scripts are plain, readable JavaScript — open them in Scriptable or view the source on GitHub. The Hijri and password widgets make no network requests at all; the status widget only contacts the site it watches.
