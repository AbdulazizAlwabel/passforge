Get an official second opinion

Saudi Arabia’s National Cybersecurity Authority checks links for free through its Tahqaq service — by WhatsApp from a Saudi number, or on the Haseen portal from inside Saudi Arabia.

Nicely Done is independent and not affiliated with the NCA. The WhatsApp button only pre-fills a message — you choose whether to send it.

How it stays private

  • Warning signs are analysed in your browser.
  • For the blocklists, your browser sends only the first 5 characters of a SHA-256 hash of the domain and link, then compares the matches itself. I never learn what you checked.
  • Nothing is logged or stored.

What it looks for

  • Known malware and phishing links from public threat lists, refreshed every 30 minutes — the ones in use are named under each result.
  • Fake versions of trusted names — Absher, Nafath, Saudi Post, banks, STC, Apple, PayPal and more.
  • Look-alike letters, hidden destinations, risky domain endings and app downloads.

Spot the scam before you tap

Most scam messages in Saudi Arabia imitate a service you trust: a parcel “held at customs” by Saudi Post, an Absher or Nafath “update”, a bank card “suspended”. The link usually looks almost right — absher-sa.info, spl-delivery.top, alrajhl.com. This checker compares links against known threats and against the real addresses of popular Saudi and global services.

If it says “no warning signs”, is the link safe?

Not necessarily. New scam sites appear every hour, before any list knows them. Treat unexpected links with care, never enter passwords or card details from a link in a message, and open your bank or government services by typing their address yourself.

Is this connected to the NCA?

No. Nicely Done is my own independent project. I simply point you to the NCA’s official Tahqaq service for a second opinion, because it’s free and run by Saudi Arabia’s cybersecurity authority.

Does the checker visit the link?

No. It never opens or downloads the page — that could expose you to the very thing you’re checking for. It only analyses the address itself.
