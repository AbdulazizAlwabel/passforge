Why it never expires

  • Many QR sites give you a code that secretly goes through their server — and stops working unless you pay.
  • This code holds your link or text itself. There’s no middleman, so it works for as long as your link does.
  • It’s made on your device. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Tips

  • Print it at least 2 × 2 cm, and test it with your phone camera before printing lots.
  • Dark colour on a white background scans best.
  • The Wi-Fi code lets guests join by pointing their camera — no typing the password.

A QR code that’s really yours

Restaurants, shops and families use QR codes for menus, Wi-Fi and contact cards. The catch with many “free” QR makers is that the code points at their own short link, so they can count every scan — and switch it off later to make you pay. This one writes your link, Wi-Fi details or contact straight into the code. Nobody can count your scans or turn it off.

For the technical folks

Codes are made with Kazuhiko Arase’s open-source QR Code Generator (MIT), using byte mode with UTF-8 so Arabic works, error correction level M and a four-module quiet zone. Wi-Fi uses the standard WIFI: format, contacts use MECARD, and WhatsApp uses a wa.me link.
