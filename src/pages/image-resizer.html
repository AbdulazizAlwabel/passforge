Handy for government portals and forms with an upload limit.

Private by design

  • Your photos are resized right here on your device — nothing is uploaded.
  • Photos from phones often hide the exact place they were taken (GPS). The new copies leave that out.
  • No watermark, no limit on how many, no sign-up.

Which format?

  • JPG works everywhere — best for photos.
  • WebP is smaller at the same quality, for websites and apps.
  • PNG keeps every pixel — for screenshots and graphics, not photos.

Smaller photos, without the catch

Phone photos are often 4–8 MB — too big to email, and over the limit on many upload forms. Pick a size, and every photo is resized and saved again on your device. Online “compressors” usually upload your pictures to their servers first; here, they never leave your phone or computer.

For the technical folks

Images are decoded with createImageBitmap (honouring the EXIF orientation), scaled down in halving steps for sharpness, and re-encoded with canvas.toBlob — which writes no EXIF, so location and camera data are dropped. The size limit uses a quick binary search on quality, then shrinks the dimensions if it still doesn’t fit.
