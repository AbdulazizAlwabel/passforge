## Platform

web

## Users
Everyone. People on desktops, laptops, Macs, Linux machines and phones, in English and in Arabic,
from everyday visitors who need one job done right now (fix a PDF, make a password, check a
suspicious link, convert a date) to developers and IT people who need a quick formatter, encoder,
hash or subnet calculator. Many arrive from a search result or a shared link, use one tool once,
and leave. Returning visitors pin and reuse their tools.

## Product Purpose
Nicely Done is a free collection of small, private web tools at nicelydone.qd.je (formerly
PassForge). It exists because most tool websites aren't honest: they let you work on your file
and then ask you to pay or won't let you download it. Success is when a visitor gets the job
done in seconds, keeps their file, and trusts the site enough to come back.

## Positioning
- **Your files never leave your device.** Tools run on your phone or computer; nothing typed or
  dropped in is uploaded (the exceptions, such as one-time secret links, the speed test and link-check
  prefixes, are explained in plain words).
- **Free, no ads, no tricks.** No paywall after the work is done, no "sign up to download".
- **Made by one person.** Abdulaziz builds it and speaks in the first person on the home note, About and
  What's new.
- **Truly bilingual.** Arabic at /ar/ is first-class, written in everyday Saudi "white" dialect,
  right to left.

## Operating Context
Visitors use it in short bursts in the middle of other work: a PDF to sign before a deadline, a
password while making an account, a link from a WhatsApp message they're unsure about, a JSON blob
from a log. Phone use is common (files from the camera roll, links from messaging apps), and
desktop users often have the tool open next to their editor or email.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Tools: PDF Studio (merge, split, sign, fill, compress, protect, unlock, redact, OCR, page
  numbers, watermark, JPG↔PDF), password and passphrase generator, strength checker, username
  generator, hash generator, one-time secret links (/share/, /s/), link checker, email checker,
  Base64, JSON formatter, text case converter, IP & network calculator, unit converter, Hijri ⇄
  Gregorian date converter, QR code maker, image resizer, speed test, iPhone widgets.
- Optional accounts: sign-up, 2FA, an end-to-end encrypted vault, profile, admin. Tools never
  require an account.
- Stack: static site generated by `build.py` from `src/` (plain HTML/CSS/JS, no framework), a
  Node/Express API in `server/`, served by Caddy on a small OCI VM. Pushing to main deploys.
- Self-hosted fonts and no third-party requests on pages; strict CSP. Works offline (service worker).
- Arabic is rendered at build time by `i18n.py`; layouts must work right to left with logical
  properties.
- Light and dark themes, ⌘K command palette, pins and recent tools stored on the device.
- E2E test suites in `tests/` (bash tests/run.sh) guard behaviour, and the redesign must keep them passing.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Nicely Done (Arabic pages keep the Latin name).
- Voice: docs/VOICE.md is binding. Plain, friendly, honest; "you"; Abdulaziz's "I" only in
  his own notes; no hype, no fear; privacy in human words; buttons say what happens.
  Arabic visible text in Saudi white dialect, with search titles and meta descriptions in MSA.
- Abdulaziz signs with his first name only.

## Evidence on Hand
- Real product: every tool works live and can be shown as itself in the design.
- Abdulaziz's note (docs/VOICE.md) and the "My promise" content on the home page.
- No testimonials, user counts, press or reviews exist, so none may be invented.

## Product Principles
1. The tool is the hero. Get people to the working tool in one step.
2. Trust is shown, not claimed: let people see that nothing is uploaded and nothing is hidden.
3. Arabic and English are equals; neither is a translation of the other's layout.
4. Fast on any device, including an old phone on a slow connection.
5. Honest about limits; never promise more than a tool does.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast in both themes, full keyboard use, visible focus, respect for
prefers-reduced-motion, 16px fields and 44px touch targets on phones, right-to-left correctness.
