My promise

Your files stay yours

PDFs, photos and passwords are handled on your device. They’re never sent to me or to anyone else.

Free, for real

No “pay to download” at the end, no sign-up walls, no trial that runs out. If it works today, it’s free today.

No ads, no tracking

There are no trackers here at all. I don’t even know you visited — and I like it that way.

Privacy & security

Most tool websites aren’t honest. You spend ten minutes working on your file — then they ask you to pay, or won’t let you download it.

That made me angry, so I built my own. Everything here is free, there are no ads, and your files stay yours.
