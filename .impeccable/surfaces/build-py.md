# Surface brief: home page (/ and /ar/)

Scope: the home page, generated by `build_home()` in build.py; its world becomes the site shell for every page.
Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: everyone (phone, laptop, desktop, Mac, Linux, developers) who needs one quick job done privately and for free.
Action: both equally, using a live tool right on the page (drop a file, copy the live password) and finding any tool fast (search).
Must keep: the live password card (new password every few seconds, pauses on hover/focus/copy).
Feels wrong if: it looks like other sites, it is plain or quiet, or it is slow or heavy.
Proof on hand: the working tools themselves; a real measured "bytes sent from this page" count. No testimonials or user counts exist, so none may be invented.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Your device is a sealed block of glass: light gets in, your files never get out. The page proves it with one real-time 3D object, then hands over the working tool. It refuses the category default of a headline over a grid of identical tool cards, and also the Aurora gradient-blob look.

OWN-WORLD: Deep ink stage (#06070c / #0f1220) and a bright paper surface for working areas; optical glass with refraction and spectral prism edges (rose #ff4d6d, mint #3ee6c1, blue #5b8cff) used only where light passes through glass or marks state, never as gradient washes or glows. Precise hairline UI panels, large tight grotesk headlines, mono for values. Recognisable with the content removed: one glass slab on ink, with spectral fringes on its edges.

STORY: The visitor sees their file sealed inside glass, understands that nothing leaves the device, believes it because a live counter shows 0 bytes sent, and acts at once: searches, drops a file, or copies the live password.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left column, about 45%: headline (about 84px), one-line plain promise, search field plus a "Drop a file" button side by side, and the live password pill below. Right side: the WebGL glass slab, about 40% of the width, with a PDF page sealed inside; it turns toward the pointer, and streams of light bend at the glass and fall back. The quiet nav runs across the top; the "bytes sent from this page: 0" readout sits bottom right.

FORM: Pinned by the user after two bolder re-rolls; position 1 (user pin). seed: none (pinned): "Not premium/modern enough: sleek, high-end, current: think Apple, Linear, Vercel, Arc, Stripe" + "Not wow enough: need a jaw-drop moment: 3D, WebGL, big motion".
Signature interaction: dropping a file onto the page sends it into the glass slab (the page inside becomes their file's name) before PDF Studio opens. Motion grammar: one choreographed entrance (Emil choreograph), spring physics on direct manipulation, everything else still.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
